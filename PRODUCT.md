# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Church and worship-team members preparing weekly lyric-slide presentations. They need to assemble a selected set of songs efficiently without babysitting each PowerPoint as it is generated.

## Product Purpose

Lyrics Slides turns song searches, links, Spotify imports, or pasted lyrics into ready-to-use PowerPoint slides. Success means the requested presentations are created reliably in the correct folder with minimal manual repetition.

## Brand Personality

Practical, focused, and dependable. The interface should stay out of the way and make long-running work easy to understand.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative complexity, unfamiliar controls, hidden processing state, and workflows that require users to wait before selecting their next action.

## Design Principles

- Make the current action and the next action obvious.
- Let users prepare work ahead of long-running operations.
- Preserve user input and recover cleanly from errors or choices.
- Prefer familiar controls and concise status language.
- Treat reliability and workflow speed as the primary measure of quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No project-specific accessibility requirements. Maintain clear contrast, keyboard-visible focus, mobile usability, and reduced-motion support.
